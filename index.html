TWISIS PHARMA CONSULTANCY

About Us

Twisis is a leading pharmaceutical consultancy firm dedicated to providing comprehensive solutions for the pharmaceutical industry. Our mission is to assist manufacturers in establishing world-class facilities, ensuring compliance with international quality standards, and achieving operational excellence. With a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we have become a trusted partner for numerous pharmaceutical organizations.

Expertise

Pharmaceutical Plant Establishment:

  • Assistance in designing and setting up new pharmaceutical manufacturing units.
  • Development of facility layouts as per WHO-GMP and other global guidelines established in 2021.

Regulatory Accreditations and Licensing:

  • Grant of manufacturing licenses.
  • Facilitation of WHO-GMP, EU-GMP, and other GMP certifications.
  • Assistance in obtaining NABL accreditation for testing laboratories.

Compliance and Training:

  • Conducting GMP and cGMP training programs.
  • Training on Revised Schedule M and other international guidelines.
  • GAP analysis as per Revised Schedule M, WHO-GMP, EU-GMP, and cGMP standards.

Documentation Support:

  • Preparation and submission of regulatory dossiers.
  • Comprehensive documentation for various accreditations.

Quality and Operational Excellence:

  • GAP identification and corrective action planning.
  • Ensuring compliance with updated guidelines and standards.

Plant Establishment Consultancy:

  • Concept-to-commissioning support (land selection, layout, machines, documents).

Pharmaceutical Licensing Support:

  • Guidance and documentation for Drug Manufacturing Licenses, Loan Licenses.

Site Master File (SMF) Preparation:

  • Preparation of SMF as per WHO-GMP, EU-GMP, or PIC/S guidelines.

Equipment Qualification:

  • URS/DQ/FAT/SAT/IQ/OQ/PQ protocol preparation and execution support.

Utility Validation Support:

  • Validation of RO, HVAC, compressed air, nitrogen systems.

Water System Validation:

  • Double Pass RO + EDI (PW) + WFI loop validation.

Disinfectant Validation:

  • Protocol writing, testing, and reports for sterile facilities.

Microbiology Lab Setup:

  • Layout, equipment list, SOPs, and validation protocols.

Audit Support Services:

  • Internal audit, mock audits, client audits, regulatory audits.

CAPA & Deviation Management:

  • Drafting Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) and deviation closure.

Change Control Management:

  • Full system setup and training for change control handling.

Risk Assessment (RA):

  • Risk analysis for production, QA, QC, warehouse operations.

Process Validation (PV):

  • End-to-end support for tablet, capsule, injection, syrup manufacturing.

Cleaning Validation:

  • Protocol development, execution plan, and analytical support.

Vendor Qualification Support:

  • Audit and assessment of raw material, packaging, and service vendors.

Stability Study Setup:

  • ICH guideline-based stability study protocols for different zones.

Pharmaceutical Export Assistance:

  • WHO-GMP COPPs, Dossiers, CTD format documentations.

API Plant (CEP, EU-GMP) Consultancy:

  • For Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient facilities.

NABL Consultancy for QC Labs:

  • ISO 17025 system documentation, uncertainty calculation training.

Lean Pharma Implementation:

  • Productivity improvement using lean manufacturing concepts.

Project Feasibility Report:

  • ROI calculation, breakeven analysis, cash flow analysis.

Training Programs:

  • Customized training on GMP, documentation, QA, QC, production.

AI for Pharma:

  • Introduction, basic training on AI applications in pharma manufacturing and QA.

Annual Compliance Contracts (AMC):

  • Year-round compliance and audit-readiness support on retainer basis.

Special Strength Areas:

  • Sterile facility establishment (Dry Injection, SWFI, BFS, LVP).
  • Cephalosporin and Penicillin block consultancy.
  • Revised Schedule-M 2024 compliance modifications.
  • Pharma documentation digitalization.
